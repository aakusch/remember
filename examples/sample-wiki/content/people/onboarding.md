# New hire onboarding

This is the path for every new hire's first two weeks. Your manager owns customizing it for your role; this is the baseline.

## Day 1

- Welcome video meeting with manager
- Laptop pickup or unboxing (shipped to remote folks Friday before start)
- 1:1 with manager to set 30/60/90 expectations
- Get added to: GitHub org, Slack, Notion, Linear, 1Password, AWS, GCP, observability stack
- Read the [Handbook](./handbook.md)
- Add yourself to the team page

## First week

- Buddy check-in daily (we assign a buddy outside your direct chain)
- Shadow your team's standup and weekly
- Set up local dev environment from `engineering/setup` doc
- Land a "first PR" — typically a typo fix or a tiny improvement, just to exercise the workflow
- 1:1 with at least three cross-functional partners

## Second week

- Take ownership of a small starter project (sized to land within 2-3 weeks)
- Attend your first on-call rotation as a shadow
- Read the [Glossary](./glossary.md) and the [Architecture overview](../engineering/architecture-overview.md)
- 1:1 with skip-level

## End of week 2

- Retrospective with manager: what went well, what was confusing, what to fix for next hire
- Update this doc if anything was missing or stale

## What we ask from you

- **Ask questions liberally.** No question is dumb in your first month.
- **Write down what surprises you.** Patterns we take for granted, you'll notice — and those notes are gold for the next hire.
- **Don't over-promise.** It's better to deliver one small starter project well than to announce ambitious scope.
- **Sleep, eat, take breaks.** First weeks are tiring. Pace yourself.

## What we promise

- A clear 30-day plan from your manager by end of day 1
- Honest feedback within 30 days
- A manager who has time for you (1:1 weekly, additional ad-hoc as needed)
- Tools that work; if they don't, you have permission to fix them
