# Company handbook

The shape of how we work. This is intentionally short — when something needs more detail, it gets its own page and we link to it from here.

## How we make decisions

- **Disagree and commit.** Once a decision is made, even people who argued the other side ship the agreed version. Re-litigating settled decisions is corrosive.
- **One DRI per decision.** If three people are "kind of in charge," nobody is.
- **Default to writing.** A short doc and async review beats a meeting most of the time.
- **Meetings have an agenda.** Otherwise they aren't meetings, they're status updates that should have been written down.

## Communication

- **Slack for the day-to-day.** Default to public channels.
- **Notion/the wiki for things you'll want to find later.** Slack search is bad.
- **Email for external.**
- **Video for nuance.** When written threads are getting long or hot, jump on a call.

## Working hours

We're a remote-first company spanning seven time zones. Defaults:

- Block your core hours in your calendar
- Be reachable for ~3 hours of overlap with your team
- Don't expect responses outside business hours; we don't compute "responsive" as a virtue
- Take your PTO. Burnout is a real cost.

## On-call compensation

Engineering roles include on-call. Compensation per rotation week is documented in your offer letter. Page volume above the per-quarter threshold pays out as overtime — track in PagerDuty's report view.

See [On-call](../ops/on-call.md) for shift mechanics.

## Equity & comp

- Compensation philosophy: 75th percentile of the market in your role + location
- Comp reviews twice a year
- Equity vests on a 4-year schedule with a 1-year cliff
- You can ask your manager about your band placement at any 1:1

## Travel & expenses

- $4k/year per person for company travel (offsites, customer visits)
- $150/month for home office or co-working
- File expenses in Brex within 30 days

## Time off

- 25 days PTO (unlimited in name, 25 minimum in practice — we measure and ask if you're under)
- 12 holidays
- 12 weeks parental leave for primary caregiver, 8 for secondary, paid

## Promotions & growth

- Promotions happen twice a year, aligned with comp reviews
- Career ladder lives in the people docs (`people/career-ladder.md`)
- Your manager is responsible for advocating for your growth; you're responsible for showing the work

## Conduct

- We have a Code of Conduct (`people/code-of-conduct.md`). Read it.
- Concerns go to your manager, your skip, HR, or the anonymous ethics hotline. Any path works.
